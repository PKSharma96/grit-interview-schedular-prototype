# The 3 Most Important Student Messages
## Verbatim Communications in the GRIT Track Badge Interview Flow

This document details the three highest-stakes communication touchpoints a student experiences during their GRIT Track Badge interview journey. Each message is crafted for maximum clarity, professional empathy, academic rigor, and immediate actionable next steps.

---

### Message 1: Slot Booking Confirmation & Live Defense Readiness Pack
* **Trigger**: Triggered instantly upon confirming an interview slot from the Student Dashboard.
* **Channels**: Email + Push Notification + In-App Calendar Card.
* **Objective**: Eliminate no-shows, provide Google Meet access, and provide transparent defense expectations.

```text
[PUSH NOTIFICATION]

Your 30-min live defense is set for Thursday, Oct 24 at 4:00 PM IST with Sarah Chen. Tap to view preparation checklist and meeting link.
```

* **Channels**: Email + In-App Celebratory Modal / Diagnostic Card + Push Notification.
* **Objective**: Celebrate success and credit Miles, OR provide actionable, constructive diagnostic feedback for a retake without demoralizing the student.

### Message 3: Academic Integrity Notice & Badge Nullification Alert
* **Trigger**: Triggered when retrospective proctoring audits or code similarity algorithms flag malpractice in prerequisite contests or interview impersonation, and the Academic Integrity Board substantiates the violation.
* **Channels**: High-Priority Email (Registered Student Email + Guardian CC) + In-App Red Banner Alert.
* **Objective**: Issue a transparent, legally sound, and unambiguous revocation notice while providing an official 72-hour appeal procedure.

```text
[IN-APP PERSISTENT BANNER]
⚠️ ACTION REQUIRED: Track Badge Revocation Notice. The AI Systems Mastery Badge has been nullified due to an Academic Integrity Audit finding. Review notice and appeal options.
```
